# The Frontier Twenty Years After the Forgotten Wars

## Summary

The setting takes place two decades after the end of the Forgotten Wars.

The war officially concluded with a negotiated settlement that brought stability to the frontier territories.

Few people now speak in detail about the conflict.

## Situation

Public memory of the war is fragmented.

Archives have been lost, sealed, or destroyed.

Competing narratives exist regarding:

- how the war ended
- who was responsible
- what was gained or sacrificed

Most citizens prefer not to revisit the past.

## Social Context

### Government Position

The conflict is considered resolved.

Historical investigations are discouraged.

### Frontier Communities

Veterans remain present but rarely discuss their experiences.

Local legends continue to circulate.

### Independent Researchers

A small number of historians, journalists, and former officials seek surviving evidence.

## Themes

### Memory

What is forgotten intentionally?

### Truth

Can historical truth survive institutional control?

### Responsibility

Who remains accountable after history is rewritten?

### Legacy

How do past events continue to shape the present?

## Participating Characters

### Elias Vance

Searching for answers he has avoided for years.

### Mara Thorne

Investigating inconsistencies in the official record.

### Teren Vale

Absent, missing, or hidden.

His existence acts as a source of uncertainty throughout the narrative.

## Active Mysteries

- What ended the Forgotten Wars?
- What happened at Blackwater Ridge?
- Why are records missing?
- Is Teren Vale still alive?

## Narrative Pressure

The setting continually pushes toward:

1. discovery of evidence
2. reconstruction of history
3. confrontation with past actions
4. revelation of hidden connections

## Completion Conditions

The setting reaches resolution when:

- the central historical mystery is explained
- the fate of Teren Vale is known
- the significance of Blackwater Ridge is understood
- Elias confronts his relationship to the past

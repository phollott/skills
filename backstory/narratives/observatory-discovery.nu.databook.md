# The Observatory

## Purpose

Establish that the official history of the Forgotten Wars is unreliable.

Introduce cooperation and conflict between Elias and Mara.

Reveal the existence of evidence hidden at Blackwater Ridge.

## Summary

Elias reaches the abandoned observatory hoping to search the ruins unnoticed.

While examining the site he discovers a partially burned military ledger. Before he can study it, Mara arrives. Each recognizes that the other is pursuing the same mystery.

The discovery forces a tense conversation that ends in an uneasy alliance.

## Boundary

### Inside

- Arrival at the observatory.
- Discovery of the ledger.
- First confrontation between Elias and Mara.

### Outside

- Events that occurred before reaching the ridge.
- The later investigation of the ledger.
- The deeper history of the final expedition.

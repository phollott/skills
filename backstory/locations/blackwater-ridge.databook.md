# Blackwater Ridge

## Summary

A remote mountain plateau located beyond the settled frontier.

Blackwater Ridge was the site of the final known military operations of the Forgotten Wars. Official records describe the area as abandoned and strategically insignificant.

Few people now travel there.

## Description

The ridge overlooks a broad valley containing the remains of military roads, collapsed signal towers, and the foundations of an abandoned observatory.

Weather conditions frequently obscure visibility.

Local maps are incomplete.

## Historical Significance

During the final year of the Forgotten Wars:

- Multiple military expeditions were dispatched to the ridge.
- Communication with headquarters was repeatedly disrupted.
- Several officer records disappeared from official archives.
- The last confirmed appearance of Teren Vale occurred here.

## Known Features

### Observatory Ruins

A partially collapsed structure situated on the highest point of the ridge.

Contains:

- damaged records
- underground chambers
- unknown equipment

### Signal Road

An abandoned military route leading into the mountains.

Contains evidence of previous occupation.

### Memorial Stones

Markers erected after the war.

Several names have been removed.

## Current State

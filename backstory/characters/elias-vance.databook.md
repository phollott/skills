# Elias Vance

## Summary

Retired surveyor living on the edge of the frontier settlements.

Elias was present during the final campaign of the Forgotten Wars but has spent years avoiding discussion of that period. He believes the official history is incomplete.

## Features

### Physical

- Mid 50s
- Lean build
- Weathered face
- Permanent limp

### Psychological

- Methodical
- Patient
- Distrustful of institutions
- Compelled by unanswered questions

## Goals

### Immediate

- Reach Blackwater Ridge
- Recover surviving records

### Long-Term

- Discover what truly ended the war
- Understand his own role in those events

## Secrets

- Present during the final expedition
- Possesses fragments of a military journal
- Recognizes a symbol that should have been lost with the war

## Relationships

### Mara Thorne

Former military intelligence officer.

Relationship status:
- Mutual distrust
- Mutual usefulness

### Teren Vale

Former comrade.
Believed dead.

## Open Questions

- Why has he remained silent for so long?
- What happened at Blackwater Ridge?
- What is he hiding from himself?

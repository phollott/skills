# Mara Thorne

## Summary

Former intelligence analyst who helped construct the official account of the Forgotten Wars.

Years later she has become convinced that significant portions of that account were fabricated.

## Features

### Physical

- Early 40s
- Dark hair
- Formal military bearing

### Psychological

- Highly observant
- Strategic thinker
- Deeply cynical
- Reluctantly compassionate

## Goals

### Immediate

- Locate surviving archives
- Identify remaining witnesses

### Long-Term

- Expose historical manipulation
- Determine who benefited from the disappearance of records

## Secrets

- Destroyed evidence under orders
- Has retained classified documents
